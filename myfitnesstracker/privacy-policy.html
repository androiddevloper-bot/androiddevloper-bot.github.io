1. Overview & Local-First Architecture

MyFitnessTracker ("the App", package com.novacore.myfitnesstracker), developed by NovaCORE, is a personal nutrition, fitness, symptom-correlation, and wearable telemetry tracker built on a local-first privacy architecture.

Your daily food diaries, water intake, weight history, exercise workouts, and symptom micro-logs are stored locally on your Android device in an SQLite Room database and Jetpack DataStore preferences, protected by Android's private app sandbox and your device's hardware encryption.

2. Information You Provide

When using the App, you may choose to enter or configure:

  • Profile & Goals: Display name, daily nutrition/activity goals, optional profile avatar, and measurement unit preferences.
  • Wellness & Nutrition Logs: Meals, barcode/AI food scans, water intake, weight entries, workouts, blood glucose logs, and symptom records.
  • Account Credentials (Optional): If you sign in, your email address and display profile are handled via Google Firebase Authentication. Your password is never visible to or stored by NovaCORE.

3. Google Health Connect & Wearable Telemetry (Limited Use)

When you explicitly grant permission through Android's official Health Connect consent prompt, MyFitnessTracker reads (and, where enabled, writes weight records) solely to power your unified health dashboard, nutrition correlations, and synced metrics screen.

The App declares and uses only the following Health Connect permissions:

  • Activity & Energy: Steps (READ_STEPS), Exercise Sessions (READ_EXERCISE), Active Calories Burned (READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED), and Basal Metabolic Rate (READ_BASAL_METABOLIC_RATE).
  • Body Measurements: Body Weight (READ_WEIGHT, WRITE_WEIGHT) and Body Fat Percentage (READ_BODY_FAT).
  • Cardiovascular & Vitals: Heart Rate (READ_HEART_RATE), Blood Pressure (READ_BLOOD_PRESSURE), and Blood Oxygen SpO2 (READ_OXYGEN_SATURATION).
  • Sleep & Metabolic: Sleep Stages & Duration (READ_SLEEP), Blood Glucose (READ_BLOOD_GLUCOSE), and Historical Health Data (READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY).
Google Health Connect Limited Use Disclosure: The use of information received from Health Connect strictly adheres to the Google Play Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell, rent, transfer, or use Health Connect data for advertising, data brokering, or credit/insurance/employment eligibility decisions.

4. Third-Party Fitness & Wearable Integrations (OAuth 2.0)

You may optionally link external fitness platforms—such as Garmin Connect, Withings Scale, Oura, Whoop, or MapMyFitness—via OAuth 2.0 deep linking (myfitnesstracker://oauth).

Authorization tokens are exchanged over HTTPS and stored locally using hardware-backed AES-256 GCM encryption via Android Keystore (EncryptedTokenStorage). Disconnecting any integration in Connected Devices immediately removes its stored access tokens from your device.

5. Device Permissions & Feature Usage

MyFitnessTracker requests Android permissions only to support features you actively enable:

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used strictly for scanning packaged food barcodes and capturing meal photos in the Meal Scanner.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly when you tap Voice Log to transcribe spoken meal entries.
  • Notifications & Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK): Used to deliver configured meal, hydration, inactivity, and report reminders and reschedule them after device restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Optional tactile haptic feedback when enabled in settings.
  • Local Media & FileProvider: Selected profile avatars stay in app-private storage (filesDir/avatars). Exported clinical PDFs or CSV/JSON files are shared only when you explicitly initiate a share action via Android FileProvider.

6. Subscriptions & Google Play Billing

Optional NovaCORE Pro subscriptions (including Monthly and Annual plans and any eligible introductory free trials) are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING).

  • NovaCORE never collects, sees, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing address.
  • Google Play shares only non-sensitive purchase tokens, product IDs, and subscription entitlement status so the App can unlock Pro features and support Restore Purchases.
  • Your membership tier (FREE TIER or PRO MEMBER) is cached locally via Jetpack DataStore for fast offline verification and refreshed automatically from Google Play.

7. Account Authentication & Service Providers

We do not sell personal data. Limited technical or request data is processed only by infrastructure providers required to deliver features you choose to use:

  • Google Firebase: Handles optional Email/Password or Google Sign-In authentication (storing display name, email address, and account UID) and diagnostic/crash telemetry (app version, device model, and crash traces) to keep the App stable.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes subscription checkout, renewal verification, and purchase restoration.
  • Nutrition / AI Lookup Services: When you look up a barcode or use AI food analysis, the food query, barcode, or meal image is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS solely to return nutritional estimates.
  • Linked Wearables: External platforms you explicitly authorize on Connected Devices.

8. Data Retention, Export & Account Deletion

You maintain full control over your logs and account data:

  • Export Anytime: Generate and share a clinical PDF summary of your health metrics from Settings & More → Export Your Data.
  • Clear Temporary Cache: Clear cached image/sync buffers inside Settings & More → Clear Cache without deleting your logged health history.
  • Reset Local Data: Signing out keeps local logs for when you sign back in with the same account. Use Settings & More → Reset All Data or uninstall the App to permanently erase all locally stored databases, avatars, and wearable tokens.
  • Delete Account (In-App): Go to Settings & More → Privacy, Security & Data → Delete Account to permanently delete your Firebase Authentication account (email, display name, and UID) and immediately wipe all local device data.
  • Delete Account (Web / Email): If you have uninstalled the App, visit our Account Deletion page or email devlopersupport@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete MyFitnessTracker Account". Verified requests are processed within 7 days.
  • Revoke Health Connect: Modify or revoke permissions anytime in Android System Settings → Security & Privacy → Health Connect.

9. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on your location (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework, GDPR, or CCPA/CPRA), you may request access to, correction of, portability/export of, or deletion of your personal data, withdraw consent at any time, or lodge a complaint with your applicable data protection authority.

10. Children’s Privacy

MyFitnessTracker is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

11. Medical & Clinical Disclaimer

MyFitnessTracker is designed solely for personal fitness, nutrition, wellness tracking, and informational summaries. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or health condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making dietary, exercise, or medical decisions.
