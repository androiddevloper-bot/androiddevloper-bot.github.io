Account & Data Deletion — MyFitnessTracker

App: MyFitnessTracker • Developer: NovaCORE

You can permanently delete your MyFitnessTracker account and associated data at any time directly in the app or by submitting a deletion request below.

Option 1: Delete Inside the App

  1. Open MyFitnessTracker and sign in.
  2. Go to the More tab → Data & Privacy.
  3. Tap Erase All Data / Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email (If Uninstalled)

Send an email from the email address registered to your account with the subject “Delete MyFitnessTracker Account” to:

Email: devlopersupprt@gmail.com

Request Account Deletion

What Data Is Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication Account: Your email address, display name, and user ID (UID).
  • Local Device Data: Weight logs, food diary entries, water intake, workouts, fasting sessions, medical profile entries, local avatar photo, and connected wearable OAuth tokens (cleared immediately on-device when erasing data or uninstalling).

Retention & Exceptions

  • Email deletion requests are verified and permanently processed within 7 days.
  • Subscriptions & Billing: If you have an active NovaCORE Pro subscription, please cancel it separately in Google Play Store → Subscriptions. Google Play retains billing/transaction records only as required for tax, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.
