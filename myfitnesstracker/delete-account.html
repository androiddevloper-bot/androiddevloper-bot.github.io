Delete Your Account

Permanent account and personal data deletion instructions for MyFitnessTracker, built on a local-first Android architecture.

How to Delete Your Account
Option 1 In-App • Instant

Delete Inside MyFitnessTracker

  1. Open MyFitnessTracker and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the More tab → Data & Privacy.
  3. Tap Erase All Data / Delete Account and confirm.
Option 2 Email Request • Within 7 Days

Request Deletion via Email (If Uninstalled)

Send an email from the email address registered to your account with the subject “Delete MyFitnessTracker Account” to:

devlopersupport@gmail.com
Request Account Deletion →
Data Scope & Retention

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Firebase Authentication Account (Deleted): Your registered email address, display name, and unique user ID (UID).
  • Local Device Data (Deleted on-device): Weight logs, food diary entries, water intake, workouts, fasting sessions, medical profile entries, local avatar photo, and connected wearable OAuth tokens are wiped immediately when erasing data or uninstalling the app.
  • Retention Window: Email deletion requests are verified and permanently processed within 7 days.
  • Subscriptions & Billing: If you have an active NovaCORE Pro subscription, cancel it separately in Google Play Store → Subscriptions. Google Play retains billing records only as required for tax, fraud prevention, and statutory compliance.
